Odom Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

What Odom does

Odom helps you track vehicle mileage and maintenance on your phone. Core trip and vehicle data is stored on your device.

Information we collect

Depending on permissions you grant, Odom may process: location (for mileage), Bluetooth identifiers for car linking, photos you attach (receipts, odometer), and app settings (including expense and data preferences). This information is used to provide the app's features.

How we use information

We use it to record trips, remind you about maintenance, support optional expense reports, back up/restore on your device, and operate the app. We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or use it to personalize ads.

Advertising

If ads are enabled in a build, ads are served by Google AdMob. We configure ads so your personal information is not used to personalize ads. You may still see ads based on non-personalized signals. AdMob's own terms and privacy policy apply to how Google processes ad-related data.

Your Data Preferences (U.S. state privacy rights)

If you live in a U.S. state with opt-out rights under laws such as CCPA/CPRA, you can open Your Data Preferences in the app. You can select "Opt out of sale or sharing" to confirm your choice. Because we do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, no sale or sharing for advertising occurs whether or not you tap that option. You can also choose "I understand, continue."

On-device storage and backups

Trips and related data stay on your device unless you export or share a backup yourself. We do not operate a cloud account that syncs your mileage for sale.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact email on the app store listing / developer account.
