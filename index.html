Odom Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

What Odom (the developer) does

Odom helps you track vehicle mileage and maintenance on your phone. Core trip and vehicle data is stored on your device and stays there unless you choose to export or share a backup.

Odom does not sell your trip, mileage, or vehicle data for money. We do not operate a cloud account that syncs your mileage data to our servers.

Information Odom collects and uses

Depending on permissions you grant, Odom may process: location (for mileage tracking), Bluetooth identifiers for car linking, photos you attach (receipts, odometer images), and app settings (including expense and data preferences). This information is used to provide the app's features—recording trips, reminding you about maintenance, supporting optional expense reports, and backing up or restoring data on your device.

Advertising and Google AdMob

When ads are enabled in the app, ads are served by Google AdMob. Odom requests non-personalized ads only, meaning we do not use your data to personalize the ads you see within Odom.

Google AdMob may collect and process device or app identifiers, IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), advertising data and interactions, crash logs, and performance and diagnostic information. This processing can include advertising, frequency capping, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and reporting. Non-personalized ads do not mean that no data is collected. Google's terms and privacy policy govern how Google handles this data, which may include sharing with advertising partners as Google describes in its policies.

For details on how Google processes advertising data, see Google's Privacy Policy and Google's Advertising documentation.

Your Data Preferences (U.S. state privacy rights)

In the updated iOS release (version 1.0, build 4 and later), open Your Data Preferences and select "Opt out of sale or sharing" to save your preference and stop subsequent banner ad requests on this device. Existing banners are removed, and on later app launches Odom does not initialize advertising when this preference is saved.

This does not undo information already processed by Google AdMob or guarantee that an already initialized SDK immediately stops all diagnostic processing. Google's policies apply to data it has already received. Earlier builds may only record your preference; update to use the banner-request controls described here. You may also contact us using the support address below.

On-device storage and backups

Trips and related data stay on your device unless you export or share a backup yourself.

Support and Contact

For help with Odom, to report a problem, or for questions about this privacy policy, email bearogleyt@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, include your device model, app version, and a description of what happened. Do not send passwords or sensitive trip, location, or receipt information.
